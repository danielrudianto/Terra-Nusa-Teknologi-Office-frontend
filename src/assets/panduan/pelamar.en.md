# Recruitment Candidates

The list of candidates and the links they use to sit the test.

Candidates are **not employees** and have no account. What identifies them is
the token itself — a long link, issued once, valid for a few days.

## Who can open it

Only the **HR division** and the **owner**, as with the question bank. It holds
personal data about people who are not yet employees.

## Registering candidates

Press **Register candidates**. Only the **name** and **M/F** are asked for.

Everything else — preferred name, date of birth, address, contact — the
candidate fills in inside the test. Collecting it beforehand is precisely the
work this removes.

### Pasting a list

If the names already exist in a spreadsheet or a message, press **Paste list**
and paste them all at once. Accepted:

```
Budi Santoso, L
Siti Aminah, P
Andi Pratama
```

A name on its own is fine; a name followed by L/P separated by **comma,
semicolon, or tab** works too. Blank lines are skipped.

### Gender

Used for the **salutation** in the invitation message — Bapak or Ibu. Left
empty, it becomes "Bapak/Ibu".

Deliberately not guessed from the name: getting the salutation wrong in a first
message to someone outside the company leaves an impression that is hard to
undo.

### Validity

Seven days by default, adjustable between 1 and 30.

Counted from when the token is issued, not from when the message is sent. Issue
today and send three days later, and only four days remain.

## Sending to candidates

Three buttons on each row:

| Button | What it copies |
|---|---|
| **Copy link** | The address alone |
| **Copy message** | The full invitation, ready to paste into WhatsApp |
| Down arrow | Opens a preview of the message |

**Copy message** assembles the full text: the salutation matching the
candidate's gender, the link, the three test sections, and the note about the
timer.

The duration and validity figures inside it are **taken from the data**, not
written in. Test packages may differ in length, and validity is decided when
the token is issued — a message promising figures that do not hold makes the
candidate feel misled.

**Read the preview before pasting.** This is a first message to someone outside
the company, and a mis-sent message cannot be recalled from WhatsApp.

**Copy all links** at the top copies the names and links of every still-valid
candidate at once.

## Reading the status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not started | Link issued, never opened |
| In progress | Opened, and the timer is running |
| Submitted | Answers have been sent |
| Accepted / Rejected | A decision has been made |

**Expired** links are dimmed, not hidden. Anyone looking needs to know the
candidate was already invited — remove them from the list and someone will
register them again and issue a second token.

## The page candidates open

The address is `terrabot.alphakonstruksi.id/exam`.

Reachable two ways: typing the token, or straight from the link. Both are
needed — long links are often cut off when copied out of WhatsApp, and whoever
hits that needs a way through other than asking for a new link.

Before starting, the candidate must confirm two preparations: a device capable
of producing CAD/PDF drawings, and a stable internet connection.

## Worth remembering

- An expired token cannot be revived; register the candidate again to issue a
  fresh link
- The timer starts when the candidate presses Start, not when the link is sent
- One candidate, one token; issuing twice for the same person leaves them
  guessing which one is live
