# Other Income

Recording money received that doesn't come from project billing.

This menu is for income outside the sale of services: bank interest, royalties,
dividends, rounding, and so on. Billing clients for project work is **not**
recorded here — that goes through Sales Invoices.

## When to use this menu

Use **Other Income** when money arrives without a sales invoice: account
interest, refunds, rounding adjustments.

Use **Sales Invoices** when the money comes from billing a client for work.

## Filling in the data

Every field on this form is required — none are optional.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Date | The date the income is recognised |
| Payment date | The date the money actually reached the account |
| Income type | Determines the accounting bucket |
| Account | The AKN account receiving it |
| Counterparty | The party paying |
| Counterparty notes | A short explanation of who they are |
| Amount | At least 0.01 |
| Income description | Explain what the money is for |

Date and payment date often differ. Account movements follow the **payment
date**, so enter it as it appears on the bank statement, not as it appears on
the document.

### Income types

| Type | For |
|---|---|
| Interest | Income from interest |
| Royalty | Income from royalties |
| Dividend | Income from dividends |
| Rounding | Rounding differences |
| Other | Any other income |

As with Expenses, avoid **Other** when a more precise type exists.

## After saving

Click a row to see its details: amount, date, description, counterparty, and
when the record was created.

The three-dot menu offers **View income record** and **Delete income record**.
Deletions are logged in the activity history under the account that performed
them.

The list can be filtered by date range. The search box looks through the
description, the counterparty, and the invoice number.
