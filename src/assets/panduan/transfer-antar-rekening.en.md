# Transfers Between Accounts

Recording money moved between the company's own accounts.

This menu is only for money moving **from one AKN account to another** — for
example, drawing funds from the main account into a project's operating
account. Money going out to anyone else does not belong here.

## What it is not for

This is the most common mix-up. If the money leaves for a party outside the
company, this is not the menu:

| Money moves to | Correct menu |
|---|---|
| Another AKN account | **Transfers Between Accounts** (this menu) |
| A supplier, against an invoice | Payments, via Purchases or Expenses |
| An employee who paid out of pocket | Reimbursement |
| An employee, as salary | Salary Slip |

The reason isn't just tidiness. A transfer between accounts **adds no expense
and adds no income** — the money merely changes place. If a payment to a
supplier is recorded here, the expense is never recorded at all and the reports
come out wrong.

## Recording a transfer

One page, five fields, all required.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Date | The date the funds actually moved |
| From account | The originating AKN account |
| To account | The destination AKN account |
| Amount | At least 1 |
| Description | A short note on the purpose of the transfer |

The description is pre-filled with **"Operating cash deposit"** as a starting
point. Change it if the purpose is different — this description is what people
read when tracing movements later, so leave it as-is only when it is actually
true.

### Source and destination cannot be the same

The account chosen as the source is automatically greyed out in the destination
list. If you force it anyway, the message *"Source and destination accounts
cannot be the same."* appears and the record can't be saved.

## Deleting a record

The delete button sits at the far right of each row and asks for confirmation.

**It can only be deleted on the same day.** Once the transfer date has passed,
the button is permanently disabled. This is deliberate: earlier days' movements
have already gone into reconciliation, and deleting them afterwards makes the
figures stop matching the bank statement.

So check before saving. If a mistake surfaces the next day, contact accounting
— the correction cannot go through this menu.

Deleting doesn't erase it either. The record remains, marked **Deleted**,
together with who deleted it and when.

## Viewing details

Click any row to open its details:

- **Transfer amount** — the sum moved
- **Transfer flow** — source and destination accounts
- **Details** — transaction date and description
- **Audit trail** — who created it and, if deleted, who deleted it and when

The list can be filtered by date range using the start and end dates.
